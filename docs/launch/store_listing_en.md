Short description:

Build a team of Siam-inspired legends and watch them auto-battle through idle waves, bosses, upgrades, and hero collection.

Full description:

GAME SIAM IDLE is a 2D idle auto-battler hero collector built for quick mobile sessions.

Form a squad of five heroes, swap front and back positions, clear waves, defeat bosses, earn idle rewards, and upgrade your team. Collect 40 heroes inspired by mythic Siam fantasy, including garuda, naga, yaksha, kinnari, warriors, spirits, beasts, and constructs.

Features:

- 5-hero auto-battle formation
- 100-wave campaign foundation
- Idle rewards capped for healthy progression
- Hero upgrades, shards, and collection
- Thai and English UI
- Server-authoritative economy planned for launch purchases and gacha
- Randomized gacha odds disclosed in-game

Launch notes:

- No PvP at launch
- No ads at launch
- In-app purchases require server receipt validation before enabling production paid gacha
