## Overview

GAME SIAM IDLE is a free-to-play Android game. The game uses anonymous/device account login at launch, game progress sync, analytics, crash diagnostics, and optional in-app purchases.

## Data We Collect

- Account identifiers: anonymous user id, device restore token, and later Google Play account link if the player chooses to connect it.
- Gameplay data: roster, hero levels, wave progress, currencies, inventory, gacha pulls, purchases, settings, and economy ledger records.
- Purchase data: Google Play purchase token, product id, validation result, and grant ledger. We do not collect full payment card details.
- Diagnostics: crash logs, device model, OS version, app version, session information, and performance diagnostics.
- Analytics: tutorial, battle, upgrade, offline reward, gacha, and purchase events used to improve balance and reliability.
- Support data: any information the player sends when contacting support.

## How We Use Data

- Save and restore progress.
- Run server-authoritative economy, rewards, purchases, and gacha.
- Validate Google Play purchases and prevent duplicate grants.
- Detect crashes, bugs, fraud, and economy abuse.
- Measure onboarding, retention, balance, and feature health.
- Respond to support and account deletion requests.

## Sharing

Data may be processed by service providers used for game operations:

- Supabase for backend, authentication, database, and Edge Functions.
- Firebase for analytics and crash diagnostics.
- Google Play for billing, purchase validation, and Android distribution.

We do not sell personal data.

## Retention

Gameplay and economy records are retained while the account is active and as needed for anti-fraud, purchase support, accounting, and legal obligations. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase/Google retention settings configured by the owner.

## Children

The game is not intentionally directed to children under the age required by applicable law. The Google Play age rating/IARC questionnaire must be completed before launch.

## Account Deletion

Players can request account deletion from in-game Settings > Delete Account or by contacting [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Before launch, the owner must provide a live deletion URL or support process and connect it to the backend deletion workflow.

## Randomized Purchases

The game may offer randomized hero shard/gacha purchases. Odds are disclosed in-game before pull. Paid purchase grants must be validated server-side before items are delivered.
